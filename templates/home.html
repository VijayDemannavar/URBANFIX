About URBANFIX

Bridging Citizens & City Authorities

Traditional municipal complaint portals are slow, opaque, and prone to duplicate backlogs. URBANFIX modernizes public problem management by embedding intelligence at every stage.

Why URBANFIX Exists

When potholes, overflowing garbage bins, or broken streetlights go unaddressed, communities suffer. URBANFIX ensures that every citizen report is automatically categorized, assigned a priority score based on public safety impact, and tracked until completion with photo proof.

✓ 100% Parameterized Security ✓ Real-time Notifications ✓ Zero-API Cloudless AI

How AI Enhances Resolution

City administrators often handle hundreds of reports daily. URBANFIX AI automatically analyzes description clarity, identifies duplicate reports with cosine similarity, flags low-confidence citizen choices for review, and predicts spatial cluster zones.

Core Capabilities

Nine Intelligent Civic Technologies

Everything required to transform civic complaints into verified municipal action.

🤖

AI Complaint Classification

Two-stage hybrid NLP classifies citizen reports into municipal categories with confidence scoring and manual review flags.

🚨

Smart Priority Detection

Calculates urgency scores from 0 to 100 evaluating danger to life, infrastructure disruption, duration, and duplicate count.

🔁

Duplicate Complaint Detection

TF-IDF cosine similarity links repeated reports for the same incident, preventing redundant inspections.

🔍

Citizen Complaint Tracking

Transparent chronological status timelines allow citizens to monitor progress from submission to resolution.

📷

Resolution Updates & Photos

Authorized administrators upload verified completion photographs and resolution notes visible to citizens upon closure.

🗺

Interactive City Map

Leaflet & OpenStreetMap spatial integration maps GPS-tagged complaints with status and urgency color coding.

🔴

Hotspot Cluster Detection

Haversine density clustering detects geographic zones with recurring issues within 0.5 km radius.

📈

AI-Powered Civic Insights

Predictive insights engine flags rising problem categories, seasonal vulnerabilities, and long-unresolved bottlenecks.

🔔

In-App Notifications

Automated persistent status alerts notify citizens immediately when their complaint moves to another remediation stage.

The Workflow

How URBANFIX Operates

A seamless 6-step lifecycle from citizen reporting to verified problem resolution.

1

Report Issue

Citizen enters details, landmark address, optional GPS, and photo evidence.

2

AI Categorisation

NLP engine validates category, assesses clarity, and identifies duplicates.

3

Priority Score

Urgency score (0-100) assigned based on life risk and public safety cues.

4

Admin Dispatch

Department officials review recommended departments and assign field duty officers.

5

Live Tracking

Citizens receive automated notifications as status shifts to In Progress.

6

Verified Resolution

Work completed, completion photo uploaded, and permanent record archived.
